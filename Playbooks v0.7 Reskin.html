The Four Mob Playbooks
Mob Playbook
Sicilian Syndicate
Old money. Longer memory.
Loose Change
$5
TURF CONTROL
1
Move
Send any number of Mobsters and/or Barrels to a connected District (Barrels may travel alone). Entering rival turf triggers a Standoff. Never enter Police turf.
BLACK MARKET
2
Unload
Sell Moonshine or Rum at a Speakeasy you Control. $300/barrel; High Society $500/barrel. Only Rum pays a Kickback (1 spent Influence back per barrel). HEAT 4+ barrels: Greed Tax.
1
Trade
At a Dock you Control, swap Moonshine → Havana Rum, 1:1.
2
Extort
Collect a flat $200 per District you Control, anywhere on the map. Once per Day. HEAT Always draws Heat.
1
Beg
Take $1,500 plus a Shylock's Mark (no limit; 8 in supply). Repay $3,000 per Mark anytime; while any is unpaid you cannot be crowned.
DIPLOMACY
1
Hustle
Draw 2 Contracts, then discard to Hand Limit 3. Once per Day.
2
Rat
With 1+ markers on the Heat Track, trigger an immediate Police Raid. Take the Rat Card (no Hustle, no crown).
EMPIRE BUILDING
1
Recruit
Hire Runners: $300 each in any Ward you Control, $400 in your Safehouse District.
1
Secure
Relocate your one Safehouse to a District you Control for $500 (abandoning its old block).
2
Rise
Boss dead? Place a new Boss in a District you Control or any empty District. Spawn 2 free Runners. May Secure there for free.
FACTION TRAIT
Untouchable. The precinct captains are on the payroll, the Sweep never thins your ranks. Your Districts may hold any number of Mobsters at Reckoning.
SIGNATURE PLAYS
2
Hit
While Pinned in a rival District, launch a special attack: if you score any hits, the rival Boss takes the first hit. HEAT if you kill.
1
Consigliere
Remove any 1 marker from the Heat Track and return it to its owner's Reserves.

STANDOFF · OCCUPIER'S CHOICE
0
Ambush
+1 Threat on the first roll. Pins the Invader.
0
Hold Fire
Pins the Invader without a fight, letting them pass through unharmed.
0
Fold
Surrender the District. Abandon Safehouse and Liquor.
PINNED · INVADER'S CHOICE
1
Open Fire
vs Rival. Roll Muscle; both sides kill.
HEAT The Invader takes Heat if they score one or more kills this fight (once, not per kill).
1
Advance
Move Pinned Mobsters (with any barrels they hold) to a safe connected District.
0
Fall Back
Invader only. Retreat all Pinned units to the District they came from.
The Invader stays Pinned and repeats this choice each turn until it resolves, there is no fresh Standoff, so the Occupier cannot Ambush again.
WINNING THE GAME
The crown is claimed at a Reckoning by the Boss who holds 10 Influence and at least 15 Respect.
Every Shylock's Mark must be repaid first, and a Rat can never be crowned.
If no Boss qualifies, play runs on to the next Day. If two qualify at once, the most Respect wins, then the most Cash plus Loose Change.
