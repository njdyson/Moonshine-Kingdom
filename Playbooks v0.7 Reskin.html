Prohibition · New York · 1926
Moonshine Kingdom
The Four Mob Playbooks
Mob Playbook
Sicilian Syndicate
Old money. Longer memory.
Loose Change
$5
FACTION TRAIT
Untouchable. The precinct captains are on the payroll, the Sweep never thins your ranks. Your Districts may hold any number of Mobsters at Reckoning.
LOGISTICS
1
Move
Send any number of Mobsters and/or Barrels to a connected District (Barrels may travel alone). Entering rival turf triggers a Standoff. Never enter Police turf.
2
Unload
Sell Moonshine or Rum at a Speakeasy you Control. $300/barrel; High Society $500/barrel. Only Rum pays a Kickback (1 spent Influence back per barrel). 4+ barrels: Greed Tax.
1
Trade
At a Dock you Control, swap Moonshine → Havana Rum, 1:1.
DIPLOMACY
2
Extort
Collect a flat $200 per District you Control, anywhere on the map. Once per Day. Always draws Heat.
1
Beg
Take $1,500 plus a Shylock's Mark (no limit; 8 in supply). Repay $2,500 per Mark anytime; while any is unpaid you cannot be crowned.
2
Rat
With 1+ markers on the Heat Track, trigger an immediate Police Raid. Take the Rat Card: no Bribes, no crown until you shed it.
2
Bribe
Buy the city, one envelope at a time. Pay $2,500 for 1 permanent Influence marker to your Reserves. Hard ceiling of 10, and 10 is the crown.
EMPIRE BUILDING
1
Recruit
Hire Runners: $300 each in any Ward you Control, $400 in your Safehouse District.
1
Secure
Relocate your one Safehouse to a District you Control for $500 (abandoning its old block).
1
Rise
Boss dead? Promote a Runner, anywhere on the board, to Boss. Wiped out entirely (no Boss, no Runners, no Safehouse)? Start your turn by placing a Boss, 2 Runners and your Safehouse in any Defenseless District, free.
SIGNATURE PLAYS
2
Hit
While Pinned in a rival District, launch a special attack: if you score any hits, the rival Boss takes the first hit. Heat if you kill.
1
Consigliere
Remove any 1 marker from the Heat Track and return it to its owner's Reserves.

vs Rival. Roll Muscle; both sides kill.

1
Advance
Move Pinned Mobsters (with any barrels they hold) to a safe connected District.
0
Fall Back
Invader only. Retreat all Pinned units to the District they came from.
The Invader stays Pinned and repeats this choice each turn until it resolves, there is no fresh Standoff, so the Occupier cannot Ambush again.
WINNING THE GAME
The crown is claimed at Reckoning by the Boss who holds 10 Influence and 15+ Respect.
The Commission: Bribe at $2,500 a marker until all 10 Influence are yours.
Respect: 2 apiece for each Borough Deed, and for the Ward Boss, Harbormaster and Night Mayor (most drinking venues) Titles. Plus the Respect printed on every Job you've pulled off.
Every Shylock's Mark must be repaid first, and a Rat can never be crowned.
If no Boss qualifies, play runs on to the next Day. If two qualify at once, the most Respect wins, then the most Cash plus Loose Change.
