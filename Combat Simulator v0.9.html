How each trial resolves
Each trial: opening Ambush (unless Stealth), then the Pinned invader spends Influence per volley until it runs out: Open Fire costs 1, Hit costs 2, Torch costs 1 plus a sacrificed Runner. At 0 Influence the invader must Fall Back (mobsters survive, liquor lost). A Plunder raid always keeps 1 Influence back to Advance out with the loot (Fall Back would drop it). Firepower is the free Irish Trait: it boosts the invader's Open Fire volleys only, never an Ambush or any defence. Heat: only the first shot counts. A firefight adds one marker, to whoever fired first: here the occupier's Ambush, so the invader who storms in draws none. (If Stealth denies the ambush, the invader fires first and takes it.) The model assumes an occupier with a Ledger marker in hand who always Ambushes; a spent-out or laid-low defender forfeits the shot.
