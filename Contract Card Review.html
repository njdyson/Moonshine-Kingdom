Contract Card Review

Moonshine Kingdom v0.65 deck — 48 live contracts (20 Gigs · 16 Rackets · 12 Scores) + 5 benched, rated for difficulty, theme & novelty. Working notes for playtest tuning.

How to read this

Diff — how hard to actually pull off and hold to the deadline (★1 trivial → ★5 brutal) Fit — ✓ matched to tier · ⚠ mis-tuned Theme — flavor & name-to-objective fit (★1 generic → ★5 evocative) Nov — distinctiveness within the deck (★1 duplicate → ★5 unique)

Difficulty is holding, not reaching. Cards are drawn & prepped in secret, but a staked Contract is public (face-up), so rivals see the job and contest it. You stake in the Shadows and the deadline is checked at a Reckoning, so a Gig is exposed through 1 Operations phase, a Racket 2, a Score 3 — essentially 1/2/3 full Days of rival interference while your staked Influence is locked on the card (one marker returns each Reckoning). Objectives are measured on the board at the deadline (no tracking), and none rely on Reckoning order, Titles, or the exact post-Sweep mobster count. Contracts pay Respect only (Gig 1 · Racket 3 · Score 5) — the cash "Take" is gone.
