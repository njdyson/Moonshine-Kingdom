Prohibition · New York · 1929
Moonshine Kingdom
The Four Mob Playbooks
Mob Playbook · Yellow
Sicilian Syndicate
Old money. Longer memory.
Faction Trait
Untouchable. The precinct captains are on the payroll. Police Squads never enter your Safehouse District during a Raid; if it would be targeted, they choose the next most wanted legal District in reach.
Logistics
1
Move
Send your Mobsters and/or Barrels from one District to a Connected one. Barrels can go alone, but aren't yours where you don't Control. Mobsters into Hostile turf: Standoff. Unguarded Safehouse: take over or destroy, no Standoff. Never into Police turf.
2
Unload
Sell barrels at a Speakeasy you Control: Moonshine $300, Rum $500. High Society venues buy Rum only, and each barrel pays a Kickback: 1 Influence from Reserves into an empty Ledger slot. 4+ barrels: Greed Tax.
1
Trade
At a Dock you Control, swap Moonshine → Havana Rum, 1:1.
Diplomacy
1
Beg
Visit Shylock, one Mark per trip. Loan: $1,500 + a Mark (7 in supply). Square Up: $2,000 clears one. Any Mark bars the Crown.
2
Rat
Trigger a Police Raid and take the Rat Card: −2 Respect, no repeat Rat, and it's yours until a rival Rats.
2
Bribe
Buy 1 permanent Influence off your Borough card: $2,000 for your 7th marker, then $3,000, $4,000 and $5,000. The last one off is the Nod, your key to Take the Crown. Boss required.
Empire Building
1
Recruit
Hire Runners into your Safehouse District: $300 each to the bank.
1
Secure
Place your one Safehouse in any Safe District for $500.
2
Rise
With your Boss off the board, place him in any Safe Ward.
Accounting
2
Extort
Take $200 per District you Control, once a Day.
1
Collect
Take $100 as this Play's spent marker returns to Reserves.
0
Lay Low
End your Day: Collect every marker left in your Ledger ($100 each) to Reserves, then take the top Turn Token from tomorrow's stack. No more Plays till tomorrow.
Signature Plays
2
Hit
While Pinned in a rival District, launch a special attack: if you score any hits, the rival Boss takes the first hit. Heat as per Open Fire: only if it's the fight's first shot.
1
Consigliere
Remove any 1 marker from the Heat Track and return it to its owner's Reserves.

2Standoff

Standoff · Occupier's Choice
1
Ambush
Only you roll, at +1 Threat: the Invader can't fire back. Pins the Invader. Firing first, the fight's Heat is yours. Spent out or Laid Low? No Ambush.
0
Hold Fire
Pins the Invader without firing a shot.
0
Fold
Surrender the District: Scram to a Connected Safe District, never the one he came from, abandoning Safehouse and barrels. Nowhere to go? No Fold.
Pinned · Invader's Choice
1
Open Fire
Only you pay, but the Occupier always fires back, free, even Laid Low: both sides kill, Runners first. First shot draws Heat: yours if he Held Fire, his if he Ambushed.
1
Advance
Move Pinned Mobsters to a Safe District Connected to this one, with as many of their barrels as you like; the rest are the Occupier's.
0
Fall Back
Invader only. Retreat all Pinned units to the District they came from, leaving their barrels to the Occupier. Ledger empty? You must.
The Invader stays Pinned and keeps choosing until the fight resolves, all inside the one Play that moved in; there is no fresh Standoff, so the Occupier cannot Ambush again.
Cooperation
0
Handshake
Shake on a deal; terms clear, resolved by Sunset. Not a Play. Break your word and take a Welsher card: −1 Respect, forever.
The card lands however the deal dies, sabotage included: promise what you alone control.
