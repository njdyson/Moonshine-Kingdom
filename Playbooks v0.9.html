Moonshine Kingdom
The Four Mob Playbooks
Mob Playbook · Red
Sicilian Syndicate
Old money. Longer memory.
Loose Change
$5
FACTION TRAIT
Untouchable. The precinct captains are on the payroll, the Sweep never thins your ranks. Your Districts may hold any number of Mobsters at Reckoning.
LOGISTICS
1
Move
Send any Mobsters and/or Barrels to a connected District. Rival turf: Standoff. Undefended rival Safehouse: take or raze it, no Standoff. Never into Police turf.
2
Unload
Sell liquor at a Speakeasy you Control. Moonshine or Rum, $300/barrel. High Society: Rum, $500. Rum pays a Kickback (1 spent Influence per barrel). 4+ barrels: Greed Tax.
1
Trade
At a Dock you Control, swap Moonshine → Havana Rum, 1:1.
DIPLOMACY
1
Beg
Visit Shylock, one Mark per trip. Loan: $1,500 + a Mark (7 in supply). Square Up: $2,000 clears one.
2
Rat
Trigger a Police Raid and take the Rat Card: −3 Respect at the Reckoning, no repeat Rat, and it's yours until a rival Rats.
2
Bribe
Pay $2,500 for 1 permanent Influence to your Reserves. Ceiling 10, and 10 is the crown.
EMPIRE BUILDING
1
Recruit
Hire Runners into your Safehouse District. $500 each, less $100 per Ward you Control anywhere on the map:

5 Free

Secure
Place your one Safehouse in any Safe District for $500.
2
Rise
Place your Boss in any Safe District, or relocate him if he's already out (not a death).
ACCOUNTING
2
Extort
Take $200 per District you Control, once a Day.
1
Collect
Cash 1 Influence marker for $100.
0
Lay Low
End your Day: Collect every marker left in your Ledger ($100 each) to Reserves, then take the lowest Turn Token in tomorrow's set. No more Plays till tomorrow.
SIGNATURE PLAYS
2
Hit
While Pinned in a rival District, launch a special attack: if you score any hits, the rival Boss takes the first hit. Heat as per Open Fire: only if it's the fight's first shot.
1
Consigliere
Remove any 1 marker from the Heat Track and return it to its owner's Reserves.

Hold Fire

0
Fold
Surrender the District. Abandon Safehouse and Liquor.

1
Open Fire
vs Rival. Roll Muscle; both sides kill. Only the fight's first shot draws Heat: yours if the Occupier Held Fire, already his if he Ambushed.
1
Advance
Move Pinned Mobsters (with any barrels they hold) to a safe connected District.
0
Fall Back
Invader only. Retreat all Pinned units to the District they came from.
The Invader stays Pinned and keeps choosing until the fight resolves, all inside the one Play that moved in; there is no fresh Standoff, so the Occupier cannot Ambush again.
COOPERATION
0
Handshake
Shake on a deal; terms clear, resolved by Day's end. Not a Play. Break your word and take a Welsher card: −1 Respect when the books open, forever.
The card lands however the deal dies, third-party sabotage included: promise only what you alone control.
