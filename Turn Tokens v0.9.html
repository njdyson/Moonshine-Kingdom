Turn Tokens

Prototype Component · v0.9 · 36 mm square

Eight double-sided tokens for turn order — two sets of four, numbered 1–4. The rules run them by role: one set runs today; the other decides tomorrow, and the set you draft tonight becomes today's at the next Reckoning.

No symbols to decode. The two sets are told apart three ways at once — warm gold vs cool silver, sunburst vs fluting, and a rounded vs squared frame — so they can't be mistaken for one another at a glance.
