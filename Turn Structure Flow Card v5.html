Turn Order runs Turn Token #1 through #4. At Day turnover, swap to the other moon phase token set.

1

Shadows

Fill barrels and open new deals

1 Brew
Roll: Turn Token #1 rolls (players + 1) Red and (players + 1) White.
Draft: In Turn Order, each player takes 1 Red + 1 White; add them for the target number.
Produce/Dump: Produce to activate matching stills — Domestic only (Gin, Whisky, Moonshine), yielding 1-2=1, 3-4=2, 5+=3; or Dump the Mash for no Liquor and no activation. Dumped dice leave the pool and cannot be Blowback. Supply empty? Dry Tap.
Blowback: Add the two leftover undrafted dice (1 Red + 1 White). Matching stills explode; casualties are 1-2=1, 3-4=2, 5+=3. Safehouses do not protect.
2 Grease Wheels
In order, stake Stash markers on Contracts (Days to Deadline). Staked markers are locked until Reckoning. Then move markers to Operations (max 5) for this Day's Plays.
2

Operations

Take Plays until everyone lays low

Take Play
Spend influence for 1 Action (a Play) or 1 Power Play.
Combat plays resolve fully before the Play passes.
Card Actions: Titles may unlock extra Plays such as Fix and Extort.
Pay + Heat
Spent markers return to Stash; if Heat is generated, place 1 spent marker on the Heat Track. House Pour Kickbacks return markers from Stash to Operations. Greed Tax: 4+ barrels in one Unload = Heat.
Lay Low
When you run out of actions or step off the street early: clear remaining Operations markers to Stash, return your current Turn Token, then take the lowest numbered Turn Token of the other moon phase for next Day's order.
3

Reckoning

Resolve simultaneously

1 The Sweep
District cap: 5 mobsters (remove excess runners).
2 Titles
Redistribute Borough Deeds, Syndicate Cards, and Ward Boss. Hold the most of that type (minimum 2); ties return to Supply. Police count as empty.
3 Contracts
Return 1 marker from each active Contract. If the final marker returns, complete it or botch it to its discard pile. Claim Commission immediately if a new completion finishes Gig + Racket + Score.
4 Endgame
Commission Card + 20 Respect ends the game; otherwise flip Moon Phase.
