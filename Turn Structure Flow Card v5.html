Turn Order runs Turn Token #1 through #4. At Day turnover, swap to the other moon phase token set.

1

Shadows

Fill barrels and open new deals

1 Brew
Roll: Player #1 rolls 1 White (Public Mash) + Red Dice (Players + 1).
Draft: In turn order, draft 1 Red. Add to Pier # (Private) OR White die (Public). No Docks = must use White.
Produce: Face-up Stills you Control matching your number fire. Yield barrels based on local Mobsters (1-2=1, 3-4=2, 5-6=3, 7-8=4, 9+=5).
Blowback: Leftover Red + White = Blowback. Hits EVERY matching face-up Still. Gin/Whisky: Downgrade cap. Moonshine: Suffer casualties by Muscle Ratio, then flip face-down.
2 Grease Wheels
Stake Contracts: Lock Stash markers on cards equal to Days to Deadline.
Fund Ops: Move up to 5 remaining Stash markers to Operations.
2

Operations

Take Plays until everyone lays low

Take Play
Spend Influence for 1 Play (Standard = 1, Power = 2). Combat must resolve fully before the Play ends.
Resolve
Pay: Spent markers return to Stash. If the Play generated Heat , place 1 spent marker on the Heat Track.
Unloads: Selling 4+ barrels = Heat. Selling House Pours = return 1 spent marker to Ops. 4+ House Pours at High Society = Favor Card + 1 permanent marker.
Lay Low
When out of Influence (or passing early): clear Ops markers to Stash, and claim the lowest available token from tomorrow's set (snag #1 to strike first next Day).
3

Reckoning

Resolve simultaneously

1 The Sweep
Remove excess Runners so no District exceeds 5 Mobsters.
2 Stake Your Claim
Re-count every Title (Deeds, Ward Boss, King of Clubs, Harbormaster) against the board right now. Whoever holds a strict majority of that turf (more than any rival) takes or keeps it. No sole leader (tie at the top or empty turf) → it returns to the Supply. (Police turf counts as empty).
3 Contracts
Return 1 marker per active Contract. If final marker is removed, check objective. Success: collect Cash & Respect. Fail: discard. (Complete 1 Gig, Racket, and Score to claim Commission Card).

Final Reckoning Commission Card + 20+ Respect = game ends. Otherwise flip the Moon Marker and begin the next Day.

Police Raids

Trigger: Rat Card + 1 Heat, OR 5th Heat. Resolve every Squad one at a time, in Town Planning Ledger order.

1. Most Wanted: A Squad looks only at Districts directly Connected within its Borough and steps toward the rival whose marker sits furthest right on the Heat Track. No marker = ignored. No Heat to chase = it holds its corner.
2. The Big Bust: If that rival holds more than one such District, hit the one with the most units (Mobsters + Barrels); ties break by Ledger order.
3. The Scatter: Safehouse destroyed & all Liquor seized. The crew flees to a Connected Safe District (never back toward the Squad); if there's none, the whole crew is arrested. No bribe, no buyout.
4. Aftermath: The Squad holds the District (impassable; padlocks any Speakeasy/Still). Once all Squads move, Heat markers return to owners' Stashes.
