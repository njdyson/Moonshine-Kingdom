POLICE RAIDS
Trigger: The Rat Play, or the 5th Heat. Resolve every Squad one at a time, in Borough order.
1
Most Wanted
A Squad looks only at Districts directly Connected within its Borough and steps toward the rival whose marker sits furthest right on the Heat Track. No marker = ignored; no Heat to chase = it holds its corner.
2
The Big Bust
If that rival holds more than one such District, hit the one with the most Barrels; ties break to the hottest boiler, most steam on its Still's Pressure Strip.
3
The Scatter
Safehouse destroyed & all Liquor seized. The crew flees to a Connected Safe District (never back toward the Squad); if there's none, the whole crew is arrested. No bribe, no buyout.
4
Aftermath
The Squad holds the District (impassable; padlocks any Speakeasy/Still). Once all Squads move, Heat markers return to owners' Reserves.
