Sicilian Syndicate
Turf Control
1 Move Send any number of mobsters and/or barrels to a connected district (barrels can travel alone). Rival turf: Standoff. Can't enter Police turf.
Black Market
2 Unload Sell Moonshine or Rum at a Speakeasy you Control. $300/barrel; High Society $500/barrel. Only Rum pays Kickback (1 spent Influence back per barrel). 4+ barrels: Greed Tax
1 Trade At a Dock you Control: swap Moonshine → Havana Rum 1:1.
2 Extort Collect a flat $200 per District you Control, anywhere on the map. Once per Day. Always Heat
1 Beg Take $1,500 + a Shylock's Mark (no limit; 8 in supply). Repay $3,000 per Mark any time; while any is unpaid you cannot be crowned.
Diplomacy
1 Hustle Draw 2 Contracts, discard to Hand Limit 3. Once per Day.
2 Rat 1+ markers on Heat Track: trigger an immediate Police Raid. Take the Rat Card (no Hustle, no crown).
Empire Building
1 Recruit Hire Runners for $400 each, in any Ward you Control or your Safehouse District.
1 Secure Relocate your one Safehouse to a District you Control for $500 (abandoning its old block).
2 Rise Boss dead? Place a new Boss in a District you Control or any empty District. Spawn 2 free Runners. May Secure there for free.
Signature Plays
2 Hit While Pinned in a rival District, initiate a special attack: if you score any hits, the rival Boss takes the first hit. Heat if you kill
1 Consigliere Remove any 1 marker from the Heat Track. Return it to its owner's Reserves.

0 Fall Back Invader only. Retreat all Pinned units to the District they came from.
Winning the Game
Titles: Borough Deeds, Ward Boss, Harbormaster & Public Enemy No. 1 (most completed Contract sets) → 2 Respect each.
Commission: Buy all 4 Power Broker Favors → a Commission Card.
Victory: Commission Card + 15 Respect. Tie? Most Cash.
