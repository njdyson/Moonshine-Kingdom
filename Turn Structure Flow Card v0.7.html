Turn Order runs Token #1 through #4. At Day turnover, swap to tomorrow's set.

1

Shadows

Fill barrels and open new deals

1 The Brew
Roll: Black (rolled yesterday) shows the Public Mash; Token #1 rolls Red (Players + 1).
Draft: In turn order, each boss takes 1 Red.
Produce: Brew = Black + your Red. Your matching Stills brew Moonshine by Muscle Ratio (1-2=1, 3-4=2, 5-6=3, 7-8=4, 9+=5). Boss on the Still: +1 Barrel (Twist the Valves).
2 The Blowback
Leftover Red + Black. Every matching Still bursts: casualties by Muscle Ratio (a Boss on the Still falls first, then Runners); Still keeps running.
3 Grease Wheels
Stake Contracts: Boss required. Lock Reserves markers = Days to Deadline.
Fund Ledger: Fill your 5 Ledger slots from Reserves (only Ledger markers pay for Plays).
4 Ferment Mash
Roll the Black die; it stays on the board as tomorrow's Public Mash. Plan today's Operations around it.
2

Operations

Take Plays until everyone lays low

Take Play
Spend Influence for 1 Play (Standard = 1, Power = 2). Resolve combat before the Play ends.
Resolve
Pay: Spent markers return to Reserves. Heat locks 1 marker on the Heat Track.
Unload (Power Play): Sell at your Speakeasy — $300/barrel, $500 High Society. Only Rum pays Kickback (1 marker back per Rum); 4+ barrels = Heat.
Lay Low
Odd Jobs: each unspent marker in your Ledger slots cashes in for $100 (Reserves don't pay). Then clear your Ledger slots to Reserves; claim the lowest token in tomorrow's set (#1 strikes first next Day).
3

Reckoning

Resolve simultaneously

1 The Sweep
Remove excess Runners so no District exceeds 5 Mobsters (the Sicilians are Untouchable and ignore this).
2 Settle Accounts
Return 1 marker per active Contract to Reserves. On the final marker, check objective: Success → add to Respect pile; Fail → discard.
3 Stake Your Claim
Re-count every Title now: Borough Deeds, Ward Boss, Harbormaster, and Public Enemy No. 1 (most completed Contract sets). The sole leader takes or keeps it; a tie or empty returns it to the Supply. (Police turf counts as empty).

Final Reckoning All 10 Influence + 15+ Respect + honor (no Rat Card, no unpaid Mark) = game ends. Otherwise swap to tomorrow's set and begin the next Day.

Police Raids

Trigger: Rat Play (1+ Heat), OR 5th Heat. Resolve every Squad one at a time, in the numbered Borough order on the board.

1. Most Wanted: A Squad looks only at Districts directly Connected within its Borough and steps toward the rival whose marker sits furthest right on the Heat Track. No marker = ignored. No Heat to chase = it holds its corner.
2. The Big Bust: If that rival holds more than one such District, hit the one with the most Barrels (the cops follow the contraband); ties break to the hottest boiler — most steam on its Still's Pressure Strip (unique per Borough, so it never ties).
3. The Scatter: Safehouse destroyed & all Liquor seized. The crew flees to a Connected Safe District (never back toward the Squad); if there's none, the whole crew is arrested. No bribe, no buyout.
4. Aftermath: The Squad holds the District (impassable; padlocks any Speakeasy/Still). Once all Squads move, Heat markers return to owners' Reserves.
