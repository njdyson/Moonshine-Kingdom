1
The Brew
Roll: Black (rolled yesterday) shows the Public Mash; Token #1 rolls Red (Players + 1). Draft: in turn order each boss takes 1 Red. Produce: Brew = Black + your Red. Matching Stills brew Moonshine by Muscle Ratio (1-2=1, 3-4=2, 5-6=3, 7-8=4, 9+=5). Boss on the Still: +1 Barrel.
2
The Blowback
Leftover Red + Black. Every matching Still bursts: casualties by Muscle Ratio (a Boss on the Still falls first, then Runners); the Still keeps running.
3
Grease Wheels
Stake Contracts: Boss required. Lock Reserves markers = Days to Deadline. Fund Ledger: fill your 5 Ledger slots from Reserves (only Ledger markers pay for Plays).
4
Ferment Mash
Roll the Black die; it stays on the board as tomorrow's Public Mash. Plan today's Operations around it.
Operations Take Plays until everyone lays low
Take Play
Spend Influence for 1 Play (Standard = 1, Power = 2). Resolve combat before the Play ends.
Resolve
Pay: spent markers return to Reserves; Heat locks 1 marker on the Heat Track. Unload (Power Play): sell at your Speakeasy, $300/barrel, $500 High Society. Only Rum pays Kickback (1 marker back per Rum). HEAT 4+ barrels.
Lay Low
Odd Jobs: each unspent marker in your Ledger slots cashes for $100 (Reserves don't pay). Clear Ledger slots to Reserves; claim the lowest token in tomorrow's set (#1 strikes first next Day).
Reckoning Resolve simultaneously
1
The Sweep
Remove excess Runners so no District exceeds 5 Mobsters (the Sicilians are Untouchable and ignore this).
2
Settle Accounts
Return 1 marker per active Contract to Reserves. On the final marker, check objective: Success → add to Respect pile; Fail → discard.
3
Stake Your Claim
Re-count every Title: Borough Deeds, Ward Boss, Harbormaster, Public Enemy No. 1 (most completed Contract sets). The sole leader takes or keeps it; a tie or empty returns it to Supply (Police turf counts as empty).
FINAL RECKONING
10 Influence + 15+ Respect + honor (no Rat Card, no unpaid Mark) ends the game. Otherwise swap to tomorrow's set and begin the next Day.

POLICE RAIDS
Trigger: Rat Play (1+ Heat), or the 5th Heat. Resolve every Squad one at a time, in Borough order.
1
Most Wanted
A Squad looks only at Districts directly Connected within its Borough and steps toward the rival whose marker sits furthest right on the Heat Track. No marker = ignored; no Heat to chase = it holds its corner.
2
The Big Bust
If that rival holds more than one such District, hit the one with the most Barrels; ties break to the hottest boiler, most steam on its Still's Pressure Strip (unique per Borough, so it never ties).
3
The Scatter
Safehouse destroyed & all Liquor seized. The crew flees to a Connected Safe District (never back toward the Squad); if there's none, the whole crew is arrested. No bribe, no buyout.
4
Aftermath
The Squad holds the District (impassable; padlocks any Speakeasy/Still). Once all Squads move, Heat markers return to owners' Reserves.
