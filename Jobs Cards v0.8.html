Prohibition · New York · 1926
The Jobs Deck
v0.8 · Breadth-First Sample · 16 Cards + 10 Drafts (objectives proposed)

Every Job is an event, not a board state: a verb, an object, and a moment. The Play is the unit — at the end of a Play, check whether that Play did the thing. Things that happened during the Play count, even if undone before it ended.
