Sicilian Syndicate Playbook
Turf Control
1 Move Move mobsters to a connected district (max 1 barrel each). Rival turf: Standoff. Police turf: Pinned; liquor seized.
1 Push Move barrels between a District you Control and one connected District. Rival turf receives them as a gift.
Black Market
2 Unload Sell at a controlled Joint. House Pour: +1 Kickback/barrel. 4+ barrels: Greed Tax
1 Smuggle At a controlled Dock: send up to 5 Mobsters from the Dock to roll for Havana Rum (failed dice = arrests; Runners first).
Diplomacy
1 Hustle Boss in play: draw 2 Contracts (3 with Texas), then discard to Hand Limit 3 (5 with Texas). Once per Day.
2 Rat If Heat has 1+ markers, trigger a Raid. Target most Heat markers; ties hit most recent. Take the Rat Card.
2 Bribe At controlled High Society: pay $2,500 for its Favor and +1 permanent Influence. Once per District per player.
Empire Building
1 Recruit Hire Runners in a controlled Safehouse: $300 each, or $200 each if you hold Ward Boss.
1 Secure Place or relocate a Safehouse in a controlled District for $500.
2 Rise Boss dead? Place a new Boss in your District or any Unoccupied District with 2 Runners. May Secure there for free.
Signature Plays
2 Capo Promote Runner to a 2nd Boss. +2 Threat together.
1 Consigliere Remove any marker from the Heat track. Return it to owners Stash.

Shylock's Mark: Take $1,500 before endgame. Repay $2,500 before game end or lose -1 Respect.

Combat Sequence
1. Move In
Enter Rival District. Turn Pauses.
2. Standoff
Rival chooses Ambush (+1 Threat), Hold Fire, or Fold. Ambush/Hold pins; Fold ends Play.
3. Pinned
Choose: Assault (1), Storm (1), Advance (1), or Fall Back (0, invader only).
Attacker kills:
Police Raids
Trigger: Rat with 1+ Heat, or 5th Heat. Target most markers; ties = most recent.
1. Precision Strikes
Each Precinct hits 1 adjacent District in the same Borough that is Controlled by the Target.
2. Biggest Operation
Highest total units (Mobsters + Liquor Barrels combined), then Target chooses tied operations.
3. Penalty
Per hit District: pay $1,000 to keep all, or lose all Liquor and suffer arrests by Muscle Ratio (1-2=1, 3-4=2, 5+=3).
4. Rebuild
Respawn destroyed Precincts by Town Planning Ledger, then clear Heat.
Winning the Game
Commission: Complete 1 Gig, 1 Racket, and 1 Score.
Victory: Commission Card + 20 Respect. Tie? Most Cash.
