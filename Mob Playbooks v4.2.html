Sicilian Syndicate Playbook
Turf Control
1 Move Move any number of mobsters (max 1 barrel each) to one single connected district. Moving into rival turf initiates a Combat Sequence (see back).
1 Shift Pay $100 to shift barrels to a connected District.
Black Market
2 Unload Sell barrels at a controlled Speakeasy at market value. Matching Domestic Preference barrels each grant 1 Kickback; Rum is wild (Kickback at any Speakeasy). 5+ of one type: shift Market Value ±$100.
1 Extort $200 per Controlled District in a Borough where you hold the Deed (once per Day for each Deed you hold). $500+:
1 Smuggle Once per Day, send up to 5 crew from a controlled Dock. Dice based on crew size: each success gains 1 Rum; 0 successes = arrests equal to Heat.
Diplomacy
1 Hustle Boss in a controlled Ghetto. Draw 2 Contracts from any draw piles; hand limit 4 (reshuffle discards to refill). Once per Day per Ghetto.
2 Rat Trigger a Raid and reset Heat. You may nudge the Raid total +/-1.
2 Bribe In a controlled High Society District, pay $2,500 to claim its Favor and gain 1 permanent Influence marker and 1 Respect (once per District per player).
Empire Building
1 Enlist Hire Runners in a controlled Safehouse (see Enlist Cost Table on back).
1 Fortify Place or relocate a Safehouse in a controlled District for $500.
2 Crown If your Boss is dead, appoint a new one in any safe district.
HQ: Optionally Fortify the District for free.
Signature Plays
2 Capo Promote Runner to a 2nd Boss. +2 Ferocity together.
1 Consigliere Remove any marker from the Heat track. Return it to owners Stash.

Shylock's Mark: Take a $1,500 Loan any time before endgame triggered. Repay $2,500 or -1 Respect.
