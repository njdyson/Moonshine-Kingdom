THE VOLSTEAD ACT

A Cooperative Prelude

1920. The Law has choked the city dry. Before you tear each other apart for the crown, you must first break your common enemy. The Volstead Act is a 1-4 player cooperative prelude to the main game. Your goal is to work together to supply every Speakeasy in the city. Pull it off, and you all win. Fail before the Law closes in, and the city stays dry.

Setup

Follow these steps instead of the normal setup. Contract and Shylock's Mark cards are not used.

Place the Federal Crackdown Tracker near the Heat Track with a token on space 1.

  1. The Law Lays Claim:
    • Precincts: Place a Police Precinct in every Borough's High Society District, and 1 Precinct in the Staten Island Ghetto (Stapleton).
    • Blue Wall: Remove all Police Officers from the game components.
  2. The Uprising Begins:
    • Choose Mobs: Each player chooses a mob. The total number of Runners available is 30, divided among players (Solo=30, 2P=15 each, 3P=10, 4P=8).
    • Safehouses: With 3 players, each mob has a max of 2 Safehouses; for 4 players, only 1.
    • Starting Turf: Each player draws a Ghetto Card, place your Boss, 2 Runners, and a Safehouse here. In a 2-player game, each player draws a 2nd Ghetto and places 3 more Runners and a Safehouse. Solo players draw 2 extra Ghettos (3 total).
    • Funding: Split $6,000 between the mobs.
    • Influence: Players start with 5 markers each, and can earn more using Bribes.
    • Turn Order: Draw Turn Tokens as normal.
  3. Fire Up the Stills:
    • Place a random Still token face-down in every District, then flip them all over.

Use the Speakeasy Circuit sheet as an order list for this mode. To secure a Speakeasy, Unload the requested Order (or more) shown on the sheet. Once an order is fulfilled, place one of the delivered matching wooden barrels directly onto the sheet's checklist to track the completed delivery.

Gameplay: The Uprising

Turns follow the standard sequence, but this is a fully cooperative game. The Police generally follow the rules outlined in The Law section of the main rulebook, with the following exceptions:

The Police DO Ambush (+1 Threat). This free shot happens as soon as you Move into a Police District.

The Rat Play is not available.

Raids

Since you can't snitch, every raid is triggered at Level 5. Raids work differently; when a raid hits a mob-controlled District not protected by a Safehouse:

  • All Liquor is Seized.
  • Arrest all mobsters in the District (including bosses), regardless of Heat level.
  • Destroyed Precincts rebuild using the Town Planning Ledger from the main rulebook.
  • The Law closes in - advance the Federal Crackdown Tracker by 1.

Raids use the same Precinct Raid Zones and target priorities as the full game.

Winning the Game

To win, the mobs must fulfill every Speakeasy order on the Speakeasy Circuit before the Law locks down the city (the Crackdown Tracker reaches space 10).
