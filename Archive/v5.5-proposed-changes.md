# Moonshine Kingdom v5.5 — Proposed Changes (DRAFT)

Working notes from the v5.4 → v5.5 design pass. Nothing final.
Each change: **rule**, **why**, **open risk**.

---

## 1. Symmetric premium map (remove Moonshine dens)
**Rule:** Remove the 3 Moonshine speakeasies. Each mainland borough holds one
**Whisky Bar + one Gin Joint + one Rum Club** → 12 speakeasies, 4 of each type.
(Staten Island still has none.)

**Why:** Channel scarcity moves from *geography* to *control* — you start holding
only **one** speakeasy in your home borough, so you can still only sell one premium
type at home until you capture/trade for more. Keeps the Lock's full tension but
**dissolves the Queens whisky-lockout** (the geographic asymmetry we kept patching).
The Queens matched-pair and rum=$500 stop being *fixes* and become optional flavour.

**Open risk / cleanup:**
- "3 of each type" only = 9 speakeasies and can't symmetrise 4 boroughs — use **4 of
  each / 12 total** for full symmetry.
- **Williamsburg** is currently a Moonshine High-Society speakeasy (Rothstein) — must
  be re-poured to W/G/R and Rothstein's Toast condition reassigned.
- With 3 premiums for 4 Favors, two Favors now share a type — minor rethink.
- Decide **starting-speakeasy type** per mob (different premiums for variety, or same
  for strict fairness).

## 2. The House-Pour Lock + Moonshine wildcard
**Rule:** A Speakeasy sells only its House Pour (full value + Kickback). **Moonshine is
a wildcard: sell it anywhere for $200, no Kickback.**

**Why:** Type becomes a territorial question, not a solved one. Moonshine becomes the
clean **floor liquor / liquid cash** — never stranded, never premium, needs no channel.
Resolves the old "moonshine orphan" problem.

**Open risk:** Logistics/table-time and stranded-premium feel-bad (mitigated by trade +
the Cover Charge below).

## 3. Keep Kickbacks
Retained. Under the Lock every premium sale matches its pour → selling is ~Influence-
neutral, preserving the post-combat / tough-contract **recovery flex**.

## 4. King of Clubs: drop Skim & Cartel → **The Cover Charge**
**Rule:** *The Cover Charge:* a rival may Unload at a Speakeasy you control (its House
Pour, full value) **only with your permission** — wave them in free, refuse, or name a
price in Cash, barrels, or favours. (Moonshine exempt.) Extort is kept.

**Why:** Pure table talk, **zero tracking**, a deliberate parallel to the Harbormaster
Embargo pointed at nightlife. The Lock constantly creates players short a channel; the
King (most speakeasies) is who they deal with. Replaces both the flat-tax Skim and the
tracking-heavy Cartel price market.

**Alt if too quiet:** *The Velvet Rope* — once/day bar one rival from Unloading at any of
your speakeasies until next day, or sell them an exemption. Meaner, more constant.

## 5. Liquor values: Rum = $500 (co-premium)
Whisky $500, **Rum $500**, Gin $300, Moonshine $200. Makes smuggling/Harbormaster
lucrative. No longer needed for borough balance (the symmetric map handles that), but
still good for activating the dock/smuggle subsystem. Watch: 2-Dock boroughs scale Rum
more easily — secondary axis, not a lockout.

## 6. Printed numbers + Moonshine caps (faster setup)
**Rule:** Still **numbers printed on the board** (fixed balanced map; random seeding
becomes an "advanced" toggle). Stills marked by **caps**, not tokens: dormant = no cap;
Tap → moonshine cap; Refine → +pink; Perfect → +yellow. Blowback pops the top cap.

**Why:** No token-seeding at setup; caps stack cleanly.

**Open risk:** Numbers are now **public** from turn 1 — loses the "what's in the basement"
hidden-info (Tap = activate, not reveal). Fine for a balance-first game, but a real feel
change. Need ~25 moonshine caps on top of 14 pink / 6 yellow.

## 7. Keep the Harbormaster Embargo
Load-bearing under the Lock (tolls cross-water premium runs).

## 8. Balanced default still layout (flavour)
Queens = central matched pair (`2×7 + solo`) for burst volume; Manhattan = wide spread
for consistency. Now **flavour, not balance** (symmetric map removed the need). Verify no
borough gets a dud production hand.

---

### Parked / rejected
Two-round draft (doubles Shadows); "all stills fire on all rolls" (bookkeeping + floods
economy); draw-2-keep-1 Tap & "The Flight" bonus (Lock already rewards breadth);
Cartel variable price track (tracking overhead → replaced by Cover Charge).

### Playtest sequencing
Test the Lock + symmetric map + Cover Charge together (tightly coupled). Keep a v5.4
control group. Watch the two things models can't measure: **game length** and **whether
the trade/negotiation is fun or fiddly**.
