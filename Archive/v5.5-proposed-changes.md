# Moonshine Kingdom v5.5 — Proposed Changes (DRAFT to sleep on)

Working notes from the v5.4 → v5.5 design pass. Nothing here is final.
Each change lists the **rule**, **why**, and the **open risk** to watch in playtest.

---

## 1. The House-Pour Lock  *(core ethos change)*
**Rule:** A Speakeasy will only buy its own House Pour. To sell Whisky you must
control a Whisky Bar, Gin a Gin Joint, Moonshine a Den, Rum a Rum Club.
(v5.4: any Speakeasy buys any type; matching the pour only triggered the Kickback.)

**Why:** Turns liquor *type* from a solved question ("brew whisky, it's worth most")
into a territorial one ("what can I sell through the joints I hold?"). Whisky's value
is no longer nerfed — it's *gated* behind controlling 1 of 3 contested Whisky Bars.
Rewards a broad portfolio, feeds area control, reinforces King of Clubs, and creates
a real **barrel trade** (a whisky engine with no Whisky Bar will deal for a buyer).

**Open risk:** Logistics tax + table time (Push/Move product to the right joint);
"stranded inventory" feel-bad (mitigated by trade). Biggest single change — playtest first.

## 2. Keep Kickbacks *(decision, not a change)*
Retained as-is. Under the Lock every legal sale matches the pour, so selling becomes
~Influence-neutral — preserving the post-combat / tough-contract **recovery flex**.
The "diversify nudge" role retires (the Lock now does that job).

## 3. King of Clubs: drop Skim → add **Cartel**
**Rule:** *Cartel (Play, 1 Influence, once/day):* shift one liquor's market price one
$100 step on the **200–500 price track**. The change **persists while you hold King of
Clubs** and **resets to default the moment you lose it**. (Extort is kept — it's the
backup income that prevents economy stalls and drives Heat.)

**Why:** Skim was a flat tax that compounded the leader's lead. Cartel is a situational,
contestable lever with great theme (the nightlife boss sets the street price) and a
Harbormaster-style negotiation magnet. The paid Play is justified because the effect lasts
multiple turns (not a one-day nudge), and it stays out of the Shadows phase.

**Open risk:** A persistent pump on the holder's own monopoly type is a slow snowball.
Governors: once/day rate limit, global prices (helps rival sellers too), reset on loss,
and the 200–500 bounds (can't push Whisky above 500 or Moonshine below 200).

## 4. Liquor values: **Rum = $500** (co-premium with Whisky)
**Rule:** Whisky $500, **Rum $500**, Gin $300, Moonshine $200. (was Rum $400.)
Considered swapping W↔R, but a swap just *moves* the borough hole from Queens to Brooklyn.

**Why:** Dissolves the Queens whisky-lockout — Queens keeps a $500 premium at home via
**Rum** (it has the Rum Club + 2 Docks); Brooklyn keeps its premium via **Whisky**.
Every borough can now cash a $500 liquor at home. Also makes smuggling / Harbormaster
genuinely lucrative. Whisky stays relevant (caps still worth it).

**Open risk:** Two-Dock boroughs (Manhattan, Queens) scale the Rum premium more easily
than one-Dock boroughs (Bronx, Brooklyn) — a secondary axis, not a lockout.

## 5. Balanced default still layout (Town Planning Ledger)
**Rule:** Extend the fixed-still ledger from the 12 starting districts to a full,
hand-tuned default map. **Random seeding becomes an "advanced / fun" toggle.**
Per-borough still *identity*:
- **Queens** = a **matched pair on a central number** (e.g. `2×7 + 1 solo`) → burst
  *volume* (~4.2 barrels/day, lumpier) that lets its home-sellable Gin keep pace with a
  whisky borough's Whisky (~$1,258 vs ~$1,306/day, ~4% gap). "Quantity over quality."
- **Manhattan** = a **wide spread** (e.g. `4/7/10`) → fires ~87% of mornings but caps at
  3 barrels. "Consistent, flexible, lower ceiling."
- Bronx / Brooklyn tuned between the two; verify no borough gets a dud production hand.

**Why:** Removes still-clumping luck, lets borough identities be balanced deliberately,
and is the lever that compensates Queens for its weaker premium access.

**Open risk:** None mechanical; just needs the full 25-district number assignment written
and sanity-checked.

## 6. Keep the Harbormaster Embargo
Left intact — under the Lock it's the toll on exactly the cross-water premium runs
(e.g. Queens shipping Rum, or anyone reaching an away-borough bar). Load-bearing now.

---

### Parked / optional (raised, not adopted)
- Two-round draft (fire two stills) — **rejected** (doubles Shadows time, buffs the leader).
- "All stills fire on all rolls" — **rejected** (bookkeeping + floods the economy).
- Draw-2-keep-1 on Tap; "The Flight" portfolio bonus — **set aside** (the Lock + existing
  Favors/Contracts already reward breadth; a blanket bonus would over-tune it).
- Titles-all-Respect streamline — **declined** (keeping the Title *plays*; just swapping Skim→Cartel).

### Suggested playtest sequencing
Changes 1, 3, 4 are tightly coupled — test them together. Treat 5 (layout) as tunable
dials, not a fixed verdict. Hold a v5.4 control group if you can, and watch two things the
models *can't* tell you: **total game length** and **how the trade/negotiation feels** at
the table.
