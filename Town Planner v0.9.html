The Day Cycle · Sequence of Play
Phase IShadows Four steps, in order, before anyone makes a Play
1
The Brew
Roll: Turn Token #1 rolls the Red pool: one die per player, plus one.Draft & Brew: In Turn Order, each boss takes 1 Red and brews before the next drafts: each of your Stills on Mash + Red pours its Muscle Ratio in barrels.Split the Batch: If your Boss's Still fires, you may also fire 1 Land Connected Still you Control.First come, first served: Take barrels as you brew. If the Supply runs dry, later seats go short.
2
The Blowback
The leftover Red + Mash: every Still on that number bursts, whoever Controls it, and kills its Muscle Ratio in Mobsters there, Runners first.
3
Grease the Wheels
The Offers: In reverse Turn Order, each boss chooses one:Take a Job: Claim one and stake Reserves equal to its Stake.Walk Away: Discard a Job you hold; its stake returns to Reserves.Stand Pat: Change nothing.Restock the Market: Once all have chosen, deal a Job into every gap. Day 1 has no Offers: turn the Market face-up.Fund the Ledger: Everyone fills 5 slots from Reserves. Only these pay.
4
The Harbormaster
Set tomorrow's Mash: Whoever Controls the most Docks names any face (1–6). A tied lead rolls the die instead.
Phase IIThe Hustle One Play a turn, in Turn Order, until the last boss Lays Low
Make a Play
No passing: Standard costs 1 Influence, Power 2, Lay Low nothing. Resolve any combat it starts before the Play ends.
Pay the Cost
Spent markers return to Reserves; a Heat Play locks 1 on the Heat Track.
Lay Low
The free Play that ends your Day. Clear your Ledger to Reserves at $100 per unspent marker, then take the top token from tomorrow's stack.
The Crown & Sunset
Take the CrownWin on the spot with this Power Play (2), made on your turn with 10+ Respect, the Nod (claimed at 10 Influence), no unpaid Mark and your Boss in a High Society Venue. Nothing else is settled.
RespectYour completed Jobs, less 2 while you hold the Rat and 1 per Welsher.
SunsetWhen the final player Lays Low, each unmet promise earns its breaker a Welsher, and the tokens taken today become tomorrow's Turn Order.

Police Raids
Trigger: The 5th marker on the Heat Track, or the Rat Play. Pause the turn.Every Squad moves, one at a time, in numbered Borough order: 1 2 3 4
1
Most Wanted
A Squad reaches only Districts directly Land Connected to it in its own Borough, never the Sicilian Safehouse. It hunts the mob in reach whose marker sits furthest right on the Heat Track. None of them on it? It stays put.
2
The Big Bust
If that mob holds several Districts in reach, the Squad picks the one with the most barrels; a tie goes to the hottest boiler, the highest Pressure.
3
The Scatter
The Squad steps in: any Safehouse burns, all liquor goes to the Supply. The crew runs to a Connected Safe District its owner picks, by water too, never the one the Squad came from. Nowhere Safe? The crew is arrested.
4
Aftermath
The Squad holds the District: impassable, padlocking any Speakeasy or Still. Once every Squad has moved, each Heat marker returns to its owner's Reserves.
