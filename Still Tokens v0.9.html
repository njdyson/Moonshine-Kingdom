Still Tokens

Prototype Component · v0.9

Twenty-five tokens, one per District. On the finished board these numbers are printed straight onto the map; this sheet exists so a plain prototype board can be played, and so the brew balance can be retuned without a reprint.

Tokens are interchangeable by number: any 7 will do for any District that wants a 7. Lay them out from the District Roster on the Town Planning Ledger. Single-sided: a Still is never turned face down.
